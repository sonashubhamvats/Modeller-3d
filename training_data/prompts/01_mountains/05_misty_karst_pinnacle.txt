Mist-Coated Karst Pinnacle
Create a tall, slender standalone limestone karst pillar with a tapered, irregular silhouette and deep vertical erosion grooves. Use muted mossy grey-green stone (#6E7B68, roughness 0.85), with subtle dark moss patches concentrated near the base and a gentle lighter stone variation toward the top. Add a few restrained, low-lying mist wisps around the base without obscuring the pillar's shape.
