Gnarled Autumn Oak Tree
Create a stylized standalone oak tree with a twisted, irregular dark-brown trunk (#3B2219) and several clearly branching limbs. Finish the bark with a rough, deeply ridged appearance and place clusters of low-poly, rounded foliage at the branch ends in warm amber-orange (#D96B27). Vary the foliage cluster sizes and leave small gaps so the branching structure remains visible.
