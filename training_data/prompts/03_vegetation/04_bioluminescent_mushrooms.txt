Bioluminescent Fantasy Mushroom Cluster
Create a cluster of three oversized fantasy mushrooms growing from a small mossy base. Give them pale cream stalks (#F5F2E0) and broad cyan caps (#00E5FF) with a luminous emissive appearance; add visible glowing gill lines beneath each cap. Vary mushroom heights and cap sizes, and use restrained light spill so each silhouette remains easy to read.
