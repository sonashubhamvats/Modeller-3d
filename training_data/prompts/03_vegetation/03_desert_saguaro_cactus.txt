Desert Saguaro Cactus
Create a tall, upright saguaro cactus with a thick central stem and two asymmetrical curved arms that rise from different heights. Use sage green (#5A7C58, roughness 0.7), with repeated shallow vertical ribs along the stems and small cream-colored spine-cluster dots spaced along the ribs. Keep the cactus stable, recognizable, and grounded on a simple base.
